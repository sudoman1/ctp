What is the difference between JPG and JPEG?

Essentially, the JPEG and JPG formats are the same and follow the guidelines of the Joint Photographic Experts Group. The only difference goes back to older versions of Windows machines, where file extensions were limited to three letters, so the "E" in JPEG was initially omitted, making it "JPG".
